# Outcomes

## Primary Outcome
Create an autonomous content engine that turns real work logged by the Personal OS into publishable Automationbro content drafts without manual idea mining.

## Definition of Done (2026)
- [ ] Content harvest runs daily from goal activity logs and system activity signals.
- [ ] At least 3 LinkedIn draft candidates and 1 Substack draft candidate are created weekly.
- [ ] Drafts are saved into the Obsidian Inbox with reviewable metadata.
- [ ] Scheduling creates actionable publishing tasks instead of relying on memory.
- [ ] G13 failures are visible through G11 activity logging and system health reporting.

## Secondary Outcomes
- Reduce the gap between implementation wins and public proof of work.
- Feed G02 Automationbro Recognition with a stable supply of content.
- Preserve final human approval before public publishing.
- Reuse G09 career strategy and G11 ROI evidence as content context.

## Constraints
- Content must be based on real system activity, not invented claims.
- LLM drafting must go through the centralized n8n LLM bridge.
- Human review remains mandatory before external publishing.
- Sensitive health, finance, and employer-specific details must be filtered before publication.

## Non-Goals
- Fully autonomous public posting without approval in Q2.
- Video production, avatar rendering, or sponsorship management.
- Replacing G02 brand strategy; G13 supplies drafts and content operations.
